Program Operator: Direct AI Automation Systems LLC, operating the Direct AI Automation service brand.

Program Name (Brand / Sender Identification): Direct AI Automation

Program Description: Our SMS program delivers (a) appointment-related messages and (b) optional marketing/promotional messages to users who voluntarily opt in.

SMS Consent & Third-Party Data Sharing: No mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS opt-in consent data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors (such as messaging service providers) supporting the SMS program is excluded from this restriction and is limited to delivering the messages you have opted in to receive.

How You Opt In: You opt in to our SMS program by selecting "Yes" on the SMS Communication Preferences screen on our website prior to booking a demo, or by otherwise providing your phone number and explicit affirmative consent to receive SMS messages from us. Appointment-related consent and marketing consent are collected as separate, independent opt-ins.

Types of SMS messages we send:

  • Appointment-related — confirmations, reminders, scheduling and rescheduling updates, and follow-up communication related to your demo or service appointment (sent only if you opt in to appointment messaging)
  • Marketing & promotional — offers, service updates, and AI automation news (sent only if you separately opt in to marketing messaging)

Appointment reminder example:

Marketing example (separate opt-in only):

• Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your activity and selected preferences.

• Message and Data Rates: Message and data rates may apply. Standard carrier messaging rates apply.

• How to Opt Out: Reply STOP to any SMS message at any time. After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further messages of that category will be sent.

• How to Get Help: Reply HELP to any SMS message, or contact us at contact@directaiautomation.com or (347) 970-7475.

Consent to receive appointment-related SMS and consent to receive marketing SMS are treated as separate and independent consent categories. Opting into appointment messaging does not enroll you in marketing messaging, and vice versa. You may opt out of either category at any time by replying STOP to a message of that category.
