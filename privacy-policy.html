5. SMS Messaging Program & Consent

SMS Consent & Third-Party Data Sharing: SMS consent and phone numbers provided for SMS communications are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Your consent to receive SMS messages from Direct AI Automation applies solely to communications from Direct AI Automation.

Types of SMS messages we send:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders (when you opt in to appointment messaging)
  • Scheduling updates and rescheduling notifications
  • Follow-up communication related to your demo or services
  • Marketing and promotional messages (only if you separately opt in to marketing messaging)
Important SMS Disclosures:

• Message Frequency: Message frequency may vary depending on your activity and selected preferences.

• Message and Data Rates: Message and data rates may apply. Standard carrier messaging rates apply.

• How to Opt Out: You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

• How to Get Help: For assistance, reply HELP to any SMS message, or contact us directly at contact@directaiautomation.com.

• Consent is Optional: Providing your phone number and/or SMS consent is entirely optional and is not a condition of purchasing services or scheduling a demo.

Consent to receive appointment-related SMS and consent to receive marketing SMS are treated as separate and independent consent categories. Opting into appointment messaging does not enroll you in marketing messaging, and vice versa.
