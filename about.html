The Story Behind Direct AI Automation

Why I Started Direct AI Automation

My name is Daniel Rodriguez. Before founding Direct AI Automation, I spent over 20 years as a Senior Cybersecurity Analyst — investigating malware infections, responding to hacking attempts, analyzing security gaps, and building the SOPs and processes that turned those findings into stronger, more resilient operations. That work spanned private enterprises and federal agencies.

Three years ago, I stepped back to take a sabbatical. I spent that time going deep on AI, large language models, and autonomous agents — discovering the powerful automation solutions enterprises were already quietly using to capture leads, eliminate manual work, and scale their operations. What struck me was how readily those same solutions could be applied to small businesses. The technology was there. The access was there. Small businesses just needed someone to bring it to them.

The more I learned, the more one thing kept bothering me. Most small business owners are still manually chasing leads, missing calls, and losing customers to competitors who respond faster — not because they aren't working hard enough, but because they simply aren't aware of the AI solutions available to them. The same automated systems that keep big companies growing around the clock are completely available to a small business. They just need someone to put it all together.

That's the gap I built this company to close.

We start with a real conversation. You tell me where leads are slipping, where you're losing time, where the friction is costing you money. Then Direct AI Automation handles everything — we build it, configure it, and manage it for you. You don't have to learn new software, touch a single setting, or lift a finger. It just runs, quietly, in the background. And in today's competitive market, having the right AI solutions working for your business around the clock gives you a real edge in capturing more opportunities and growing your business.
